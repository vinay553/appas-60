MADRAS · SUNDAY, NOVEMBER 13, 1966 · PAGE 1

28 lives lost in Aliyar floods

Express News Service · COIMBATORE, Nov. 12

Best-effort transcription of the lead report. Names and numbers that could not be resolved are marked [unclear]. The separate flood dispatches beneath this report are not included.

Twenty-eight persons, of whom 8 were women and 17 were children were washed away in the worst flood disaster in Aliyar river, about 50 miles from here, on Thursday when Aliyar recorded 12 inches of rainfall.

The victims were residing in ten huts at Navamalai, in Pollachi taluk, near the Aliyar dam site.

The sudden floods were caused by the Aliyar river taking a new course due to heavy rains, Mr. K. Bhaskara, Superintendent of Police, told newsmen here today after a visit to the spot.

Mr. Bhaskara said that the grim tragedy overtook the people unawares when water gushed through their dwellings.

Among the dead was an [unclear] teacher who was visiting labourer parents of his [unclear] and the people in and around her hut. Even entreaties to move to safer places on elevated areas [unclear].

Mr. Bhaskara said that a nursery also was completely swept away, along with [unclear] huts.

So far, four bodies have been recovered. Search for the remaining bodies is in progress.

The Government has arranged relief measures on receipt of news, which was delayed on account of interruption of communications from the dam site.
